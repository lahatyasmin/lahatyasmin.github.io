Yasmin OS

A private personal assistant for tasks and household logistics.

About

Yasmin OS is a personal productivity system operated by Yasmin Lahat. It helps its owner capture tasks, set reminders and keep track of household matters, through a private web app and a WhatsApp assistant.

The service is for personal use by its owner only. It is not offered to the public and does not send marketing or promotional messages.

WhatsApp messaging

  • Messages are exchanged only with the owner's own verified phone number.
  • The assistant replies to messages the owner sends and confirms actions such as adding a task or a reminder.
  • No bulk, marketing or unsolicited messages are sent.

Privacy

  • Data is used only to provide the service to its owner. It is never sold or shared for advertising.
  • Message content is retained for up to 12 months and then deleted.
  • Messages are processed by the service's technology providers (hosting, messaging and AI) solely to operate the service.
  • The assistant does not store passwords, card numbers, ID numbers or other secrets.
